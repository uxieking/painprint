PAINPRINT

Make the invisible
understandable.

Pain doesn't show on the outside – and at the doctor's office it's easy to forget, downplay or overstate it. PAINPRINT is a pain diary you draw: mark exactly where it hurts, how much, when and why – and bring the whole story to your next appointment.

  • Draw precisely on a zoomable body – front and back
  • Pain scale 1–10 in color, from violet to orange and red
  • Log when it happened and why
  • See your trend and download a PDF for your doctor

Best on your phone: uxieking.github.io/painprint
